How to complete your registration

  1. On the Book a Slot tab, review the calendar and pick a date marked Available (green).
  2. Fill in your centre details: preschool name, centre type (KDL / ELFA), teacher-in-charge, contact number, email, number of children and number of adults / chaperones.
  3. Choose your preferred time slot from the three options for that date.
  4. Note any special requirements (e.g. wheelchair access) in the Notes field.
  5. Submit. Your booking appears immediately on the shared calendar for all centres.
  6. A confirmation email will be sent by the Crestar admin team within 5 working days.
Please note: Only one centre may be hosted per date, and only one timing per date. Slot allocation is first-come, first-served, capped at 13 centres. Transport to and from TridentAir Factory is to be arranged by individual preschools. Submission does not guarantee your selected slot until confirmed by Crestar admin.
